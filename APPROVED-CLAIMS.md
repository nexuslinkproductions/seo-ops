# Approved Claims

Pre-publication gate for client reports and social content. Nothing is
published without passing this list.

## May claim (with evidence)

- Measured before/after deltas from our own audit, Search Console, and probe
  data, dated and method-stated.
- Issue counts resolved by severity and category.
- Indexed pages gained/lost, schema validity, hreflang reciprocity fixes.
- Citation rate and share of voice from fixed-prompt probes, labeled as
  internal probe data, never as official statistics.
- Core Web Vitals improvements from lab and field data.

## May not claim

- "Guaranteed" rankings, snippets, citations, or revenue.
- Causation from a single month or single change without trend windows and
  confounder notes.
- Probe data as search share, traffic, or official statistics.
- Third-party percentages (BrightEdge, Ahrefs, Similarweb, StatCounter) as
  verified fact; attribute as "as reported by X."
- Any Swiss/EU legal statement without counsel review.

## Review gate

Every report or post is checked against this list before publishing, by an
independent reviewer (not the author of the change).
